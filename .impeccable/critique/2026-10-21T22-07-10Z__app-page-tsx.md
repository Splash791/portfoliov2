## Overall Impression

The design *system* is genuinely well-built and well-documented — better than most portfolios at this stage. But two things undercut it badly enough to matter more than any visual polish: the globe (this system's own named "signature component") gives a visitor zero reason to realize it's interactive, and the site's entire contact flow is currently dead. For a site in active use for a live job search, that second one is the whole point of the page not working.

## What's Working

- **Reduced-motion engineering is unusually rigorous.** `useReducedMotionSafe()` is built on `useSyncExternalStore` specifically to dodge a hydration-mismatch bug that was reproduced live and documented — visible again in `Hero.tsx`'s remount-on-resolved-value trick and in three independently-gated motion features inside the globe itself (autorotate, arc-dash animation, orbit controls). This is real craft-as-evidence for the technical-peer audience.
- **The Experience rework (just shipped this session) reads well.** Every entry's summary is now always visible, with only the detail bullets behind Expand/Collapse, `aria-expanded` wired correctly, and a reduced-motion `duration: 0` branch on the height animation. Confirmed in the live render: 4/4 summaries visible, 3 "Expand" + 1 "Collapse" (Deloitte, expanded by default) — exactly the intended pattern.
- **The project modal degrades honestly.** Every optional block (stack, metrics, architecture, video, screenshots, links) is independently guarded and verified to render nothing when its data is absent, rather than showing an empty box or faking content.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] No working contact flow on a site in active use for a live job search.**
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md's stated purpose is "gets a visitor to take the next step — reach out." Right now nothing does. `components/connect/Connect.tsx` is fully built (LinkedIn + email CTAs) but not imported anywhere — confirmed via repo grep and `git log`: it was deliberately removed from `app/page.tsx` in commit `aae17fd`, "per user request." The only remaining contact point is Footer's LinkedIn link, and it's still `href="#"` (a TODO placeholder since the original build, never filled in). Hero's own "Connect on LinkedIn" button is the same unresolved `href="#"`.
- **Fix:** this is a decision, not a bug — Connect was removed on purpose once already, so I'm not reinstating it without checking first. See the question below.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] The globe — this system's own named "signature component" — has no interaction affordance.**
- **Why it matters:** DESIGN.md calls this the system's signature moment. As shipped: no caption, no legend, no visible instruction anywhere in the DOM (confirmed: the only text nodes near `#travel` are the "Travel" heading and the loading/fallback strings). Discovery depends entirely on a desktop-only `cursor: pointer` swap on hover (confirmed via grep: zero `aria-*`, `role`, `tabIndex`, or keyboard handlers in `TravelGlobe.tsx`/`Travel.tsx`) — invisible on touch, and unreachable by keyboard or screen reader entirely. Two of three trips (`japan`, `london` in `data/trips.ts`) also have an empty `routes: []`, so even a visitor who does click sees one dot with no arc for most of the content.
- **Fix:** add a visible instruction line ("Tap a pin to explore each trip") plus a small color-keyed legend near the globe that works identically on touch and desktop, independent of hover.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Forced full-viewport snap scrolling with no way to skip ahead.**
- **Why it matters:** `app/layout.tsx`'s `snap-y snap-mandatory` (confirmed) forces a full-section hop on every scroll gesture with no nav/anchors — directly working against PRODUCT.md's own "recruiters scan quickly" operating context, and compounding the missing-Connect problem by removing any way to check whether there's more content past Writing.
- **Fix:** relax to proximity snap (`snap-y` without `-mandatory`), or add a minimal non-nav position indicator (e.g. a dot column) without reintroducing the explicitly-removed nav bar.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] The travel-map blog post is now stale against the feature it describes.**
- **Why it matters:** `content/blog/building-the-travel-map.mdx` describes "no real-earth texture... a flat stylized sphere," but the live globe now uses a photographic Earth texture + bump map at a different camera altitude, per a later direct request. This post exists specifically to demonstrate technical depth to the peer audience; a peer who reads it and checks the live section will find a mismatch.
- **Fix:** update the post to match the current implementation, or add a dated addendum noting the texture swap.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

**[P3] Placeholder density in Projects reads as incomplete rather than intentional.**
- **Why it matters:** 3 of 4 project cards show generic "Project summary goes here..." copy directly beside the one real, specific FarmOS card, with nothing distinguishing "more detail coming" from "abandoned."
- **Fix:** give placeholder cards a distinct "write-up in progress" visual treatment.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (cold first-timer, clicking from a resume/LinkedIn link):** Lands on a strong Hero, scrolls through 5 more full-viewport sections with no map of what's ahead, likely never discovers a globe pin is clickable (no instruction, no legend), and reaches the end — Writing → Footer — exactly primed to act, and finds a dead LinkedIn link and no email.

**Casey (mobile):** The globe's *only* interaction cue (the desktop cursor-pointer hover swap) doesn't exist on touch at all — zero discoverability on the device type most likely to open a shared link. (Cursor.tsx and Magnetic.tsx correctly gate on `pointer: fine` and disable cleanly on touch — that part's done right.)

**Sam (accessibility):** Confirmed via grep: zero ARIA/keyboard attributes in `TravelGlobe.tsx`/`Travel.tsx`. `onPointClick` wires only mouse-raycast events against the WebGL canvas — there's no keyboard-focusable proxy (e.g. an sr-only list of trip buttons) anywhere in source. A keyboard-only user cannot reach a single pin; the Travel section's one piece of real interactive content is mouse/touch-only.

## Minor Observations

- `data/experience.ts` has a typo in real content: "Goverment and Public Services" → "Government."
- `DESIGN.md`'s own Travel description ("frosted-looking 3D sphere") is stale against the photographic Earth texture — worth a pass alongside the blog-post fix above.
- Footer's plain-text LinkedIn link has different visual weight than Hero's pill CTA — even once contact is fixed, align the two.
- Pin colors rotate by trip-array index (`ti % 3`) rather than a fixed per-trip assignment — harmless at exactly 3 trips, will silently reuse colors on a 4th.
- `controls.enableZoom = false` was a deliberate fix for a real clipping bug (documented in CLAUDE.md) — fine to keep, but nothing on-screen explains zoom is intentionally locked.
